# Privacy

StreamLite is built to keep your data on your device.

- **No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting.**
- Your history, favorites, resume positions, custom headers and settings are stored only in the app's private storage.
- The app connects only to the links you open. Online subtitle search sends the search text you enter to the subtitle provider.
- The **Check for updates** button contacts api.github.com only when you tap it.
- Data-usage history is counted on the device and never uploaded.
- The debug report is created only when you tap it, and is shared only if you paste it somewhere yourself.
- Permissions: `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` (to detect reconnects), and, for background audio only, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` (the playback notification) and `WAKE_LOCK` (keeps playback running with the screen off). Nothing else.
- Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.
